Summary
-------
- VerseStore wraps chromadb.PersistentClient with cosine HNSW collections.
- ingest_corpus.py embeds all KJV (31,102) and WEB (31,098) verses in 256-item
  chunks, upserts idempotently, and records one corpus_ingest summary row per
  translation only after the collection count matches the manifest.
- --max-batches 1 produces a partial collection (256 verses) and zero
  corpus_ingest rows; a subsequent full run completes the collection.
- Semantic query for "love your enemies" returns Matt 5:44, Luke 6:27, and
  Luke 6:35 in the top-5 KJV hits.
